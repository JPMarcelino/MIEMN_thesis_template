\section{Background}

This section sets the stage, raises the stakes, and provides the
background and motivation for the research. It discusses the problem
statement, the objectives, and why anyone outside this room should care.

\subsection{Problem Statement}

Describe the specific problem or challenge that your research addresses.
State clearly where current knowledge ends and confident guessing begins.

\subsection{Research Objectives}

List the main objectives of your research. These should be specific,
measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART), or at least
look that way in a bulleted list.

applications, ideally in three parallel clauses.
